OPEN INTEREST / Daily brief, format v2. Friday, 27 November 2026.

LEAD: Binance says its withdrawal suspension is over, 28 days after the theft.

The exchange declared its recovery work complete on 26 November 2026. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/95c463396a034f1ab3994814225e6d69] Some networks stayed paused, and Binance says those pauses are routine. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/95c463396a034f1ab3994814225e6d69]

Binance's notice says the suspension imposed after its October security incident "has been fully lifted". [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/95c463396a034f1ab3994814225e6d69] Unchained counts 28 days between the 29 October 2026 breach and the notice. [https://unchainedcrypto.com/binance-lifts-withdrawal-suspension-from-6-3-billion-hack-says-recovery-is-complete/] Binance began reopening withdrawals in phases on 1 November 2026, Unchained reports. [https://unchainedcrypto.com/binance-lifts-withdrawal-suspension-from-6-3-billion-hack-says-recovery-is-complete/]

The loss was about $6.3 billion by Binance's account, as Unchained relays it. [https://unchainedcrypto.com/binance-lifts-withdrawal-suspension-from-6-3-billion-hack-says-recovery-is-complete/] Binance said on 13 November 2026 that it had put up more than $6.34 billion of its own money to cover it, according to the same report. [https://unchainedcrypto.com/binance-lifts-withdrawal-suspension-from-6-3-billion-hack-says-recovery-is-complete/]

The notice carries a qualification: withdrawals on "a small number of networks" remain paused for maintenance or planned upgrades. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/95c463396a034f1ab3994814225e6d69] Binance calls this separate from the recovery and does not name the networks. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/95c463396a034f1ab3994814225e6d69]

Unchained checked Binance's status listings early on 27 November 2026. It found USDT withdrawals on Solana blocked under the label "Low Hot Wallet Balance". [https://unchainedcrypto.com/binance-lifts-withdrawal-suspension-from-6-3-billion-hack-says-recovery-is-complete/] Sonic withdrawals were halted under "Security Issues", and USDC carried the low-balance label on Hedera and World Chain. [https://unchainedcrypto.com/binance-lifts-withdrawal-suspension-from-6-3-billion-hack-says-recovery-is-complete/]

Binance repeats that customer assets are backed at least 1:1 and points users to its reserves site. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/95c463396a034f1ab3994814225e6d69] That is the company's claim, and the notice offers no outside verification of it. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/95c463396a034f1ab3994814225e6d69] The notice also warns that nobody from Binance will offer to "speed up withdrawals". [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/95c463396a034f1ab3994814225e6d69]

Trail of Bits summarised Binance's 3 November 2026 report as describing social engineering, misuse of an internal AI tool, stolen credentials and access to the wallet-signing service. [https://blog.trailofbits.com/2026/11/06/after-binance-check-what-your-signing-systems-can-approve/] The notice says nothing about what changed in those systems. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/95c463396a034f1ab3994814225e6d69]

NEWS:

[NEWS] Braiins says an attacker rerouted bitcoin miners to an impostor pool.
The mining company published its account on 25 November 2026. [https://braiins.com/blog/bitcoin-hashrate-hijacking-unencrypted-stratum-v1] It says traffic between miners and pools was rerouted on 20 November 2026, cutting off more than a thousand mining sites in the same second. [https://braiins.com/blog/bitcoin-hashrate-hijacking-unencrypted-stratum-v1] Braiins blames Stratum V1, a protocol from 2012 that sends data unencrypted, and says its own pool was not breached. [https://braiins.com/blog/bitcoin-hashrate-hijacking-unencrypted-stratum-v1] A 2014 attack of the same kind took about $83,000, and Braiins says the technique now runs at scale. [https://braiins.com/blog/bitcoin-hashrate-hijacking-unencrypted-stratum-v1]

[NEWS] Zodia Custody says an intruder reached client contact details.
Chief executive Richard Clark said the firm detected attempts to probe its platform on 23 November 2026. [https://zodia-custody.com/statement-on-recent-security-incident/] On 24 November 2026 its monitoring found that an unauthorised party had accessed limited client information. [https://zodia-custody.com/statement-on-recent-security-incident/] Clark said the data was public organisation details plus business names and emails for a small number of clients and employees. [https://zodia-custody.com/statement-on-recent-security-incident/] The firm reports no unauthorised transactions so far and gives no count of those affected. [https://zodia-custody.com/statement-on-recent-security-incident/]

[TECH] Vitalik Buterin argues that secure hardware must be open down to its geometry.
Buterin published a primer on open-source chip design on 26 November 2026. [https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/11/26/a_shallow_dive_into_open-source_chip_design.html] He lists three fabrication plants that publish their design kits without confidentiality agreements, in Singapore, Minnesota and Frankfurt an der Oder. [https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/11/26/a_shallow_dive_into_open-source_chip_design.html] He argues that a chip can be checked against its design only if the physical layout file is public. [https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/11/26/a_shallow_dive_into_open-source_chip_design.html] He expects such hardware to be perhaps ten times less efficient than the fastest chips, and proposes a slogan: "not your silicon, not your keys". [https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2026/11/26/a_shallow_dive_into_open-source_chip_design.html]
